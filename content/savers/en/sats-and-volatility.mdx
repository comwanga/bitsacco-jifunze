Juma hears that one bitcoin costs millions of shillings and decides it is not for him. But nobody has to buy a whole bitcoin, just as nobody has to buy a whole cow to drink milk.

## Sats: the small change of Bitcoin

One bitcoin is divided into 100,000,000 (one hundred million) <Term id="sats" />. Most people save and pay in sats.

Here is an example, using a made-up price that is **not** today's price:

| If 1 bitcoin costs | then 100 shillings buys |
| --- | --- |
| 10,000,000 KES | 1,000 sats |
| 5,000,000 KES | 2,000 sats |

The working: 100 ÷ 10,000,000 × 100,000,000 = 1,000 sats.

## Why the price moves

The price of bitcoin in shillings emerges from trades. Every completed trade has both a buyer and a seller; stronger buying or selling pressure at the available prices can move the next trade's price. Demand, available supply, liquidity, news and expectations all matter. Big, fast moves up and down are called <Term id="volatility" />.

Bitcoin's price in shillings can change sharply. A shilling remains one shilling as a unit of account, but its purchasing power and foreign-exchange value also change. Over a week, month or year, the shilling value of your sats can fall a lot, as well as rise. A long holding period does not guarantee recovery or profit.

## Shillings and Bitcoin side by side

| | Shillings | Bitcoin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who issues it | Central bank cash; commercial banks also create deposit money through lending | Miners claim new issuance within rules checked by nodes |
| How much can exist | Influenced by monetary policy, lending and financial constraints | About 21 million under current consensus rules |
| Price in shillings | Always 1 | Changes every minute |
| Examples of risk | <Term id="inflation">Inflation</Term>, provider failure or access restrictions, depending on how it is held | Price losses at any horizon; key loss, theft and access problems |

<Callout type="warning" title="Questions to ask before you save in Bitcoin">
When will I need this money? Could I wait if the price fell for a long time? Do I understand who holds the keys? Jifunze can't answer these for you, and nobody who promises a guaranteed return can be trusted.
</Callout>
