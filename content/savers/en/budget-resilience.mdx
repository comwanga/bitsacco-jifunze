## Saving starts with a purpose

Saving means keeping resources for later use. It can support rent, school, a business repair or retirement. Spending on food or health is not a moral failure, and low income can leave little to save. A useful budget describes reality before setting a target. Bitcoin is one possible instrument to evaluate after the purpose, timing and risks are understood.

Income is money received; an expense pays for something consumed or owed. Moving 500 from a current account to a savings account changes its location, not household income. Repaying a loan uses cash even when only its interest is treated as an expense in other kinds of accounts. For a household cash-flow plan, include the entire payment due.

## Case: Amina's repair reserve

In this fictional month, Amina starts with 2,000 local currency units. She expects 12,000 on day 5 and 8,000 on day 20. Rent of 8,000 is due on day 3; food and transport total 7,000; debt payments are 3,000; and a planned annual bill needs 2,000 set aside.

Her monthly plan shows 2,000 + 20,000 − 8,000 − 7,000 − 3,000 − 2,000 = **2,000 remaining**. Yet on day 3 she has only 2,000 against rent of 8,000: a **6,000 timing gap**. A positive monthly total does not make every due date affordable. She must investigate timing, spending or support options before promising a new contribution. No calculator can create missing income.

Write income and payment dates on a calendar. Use a cautious income scenario when work is irregular, then compare it with an ordinary month. Record actual spending, including seasonal costs and support to relatives. A planned school bill belongs in a goal fund; an unexpected essential repair may belong in an emergency reserve. Neither category has a universal required size.

## Access matters as much as a balance

Liquidity means being able to meet a payment when it is due, without an unacceptable delay or loss. Compare instruments using that need:

| Instrument | Useful property | Question to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash | Works without a provider login | Theft, fire and safe physical access? |
| Bank or cooperative deposit | Records and familiar payment services | Withdrawal terms, failure risk and applicable protection? |
| Mobile-money balance | Convenient local payments | Agent cash, limits, outages and account recovery? |
| Fixed-term saving | Known contractual terms | Can it be accessed early, and at what cost? |
| Bitcoin | Transferable without a conventional account in self-custody | Price loss, fees, key access and a usable route to the expense currency? |

A reserve that requires selling Bitcoin during a price fall may deliver less than the bill needs. A deposit locked until next year may be unsuitable for tomorrow's repair. Local deposit protection, where it exists, has eligibility rules and limits; never assume it covers Bitcoin or every provider.

## Try it

On paper, place Amina's payments on dates you choose and calculate the running balance after each. Then delay her second income by ten days. Identify the first shortfall and one information gap to investigate. Keep this fictional worksheet; do not enter real account details. **Worked check:** the original monthly remainder is 2,000, while the early rent gap is 6,000. They measure different problems.
