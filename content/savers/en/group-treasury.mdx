## Start with obligations and authority

A treasury is the pool of assets managed for a group's purpose. It is not automatically money that officials can invest however they like. Identify ownership, member claims, expected payouts, restricted social funds and loans. A Bitcoin experiment needs informed authorization under the group's rules and any applicable law. Member enthusiasm or a signing threshold is not sufficient evidence of permission.

A useful policy states its purpose, permitted activities, responsible roles, custody requirements, reporting unit, acceptable costs and review date. It also states when **not** to proceed: essential near-term commitments, unclear authority, members unable to understand or consent, unreliable recovery or unverified service terms. A paper-only experiment is a valid outcome; no lesson requires buying Bitcoin.

## Case: the school-term fund

Fictional Tumaini has 100,000 local units of assets: 70,000 immediately available in the expense currency and Bitcoin initially quoted at 30,000. Members expect school payouts of 80,000 next week. Assume Bitcoin falls 50% and conversion costs 1,000. Selling it would yield 14,000, giving total usable resources of **84,000** and only **4,000** above the promised payout, before other obligations.

Now the conversion service stops withdrawals for ten days. Only 70,000 is available before the deadline, a **10,000 shortfall**, even though quoted assets total 85,000 before selling costs. These are fictional stress assumptions, not a suggested allocation or an acceptable risk limit. A group responsible for critical payments may conclude the experiment is inappropriate.

## Keep two linked sets of records

The asset ledger records actual local currency, sats, loans and fees in their own units. The member ledger records contributions, shares or payout claims under agreed rules. Periodic reports explain the exchange source and time, realized proceeds versus unrealized valuation changes, outstanding loans, costs and restrictions. If promised claims exceed available assets, a high sats balance cannot erase that difference.

Agree how an arriving or departing member is valued, which costs apply and when settlement is due. Avoid shifting yesterday's loss to a new member through an unexplained exchange rate. A loan expected next month does not meet a withdrawal due today. Reconcile balances against independent evidence and preserve corrections.

## Transparency with boundaries

Members need understandable totals, liabilities, expenses, decisions and independent review. They do not necessarily need every member's personal circumstances or a publicly traceable treasury address. A public wallet can reveal social connections and expose officials to targeting. Controlled reports and independent reconciliation can provide accountability while reducing disclosure. On-chain assets alone do not prove the group has no hidden liabilities.

## Rehearse an institutional failure

Assign who pauses new activity, checks facts, preserves records, informs members, protects secret material and convenes a valid decision. Test missing signers, dishonest officials, an unavailable conversion service and a member demanding an urgent exit. Do not promise to reverse a valid Bitcoin transfer. Distinguish a temporary service outage from key compromise; the response may differ.

## Try it

Draft a fictional policy with purpose, obligations, authorized decisions, reporting, exit treatment and stop conditions. Run Tumaini's price-drop case and outage case. **Worked check:** 84,000 after the assumed sale, but only 70,000 timely during the outage. Explain why majority enthusiasm does not authorize hiding that shortfall or overriding a member's existing rights.
