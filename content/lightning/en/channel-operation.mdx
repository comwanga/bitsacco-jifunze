While a channel is open, the two nodes keep adding and removing <Term id="htlc">HTLCs</Term> and signing new commitments: that is payment forwarding. Sooner or later, a channel ends.

## Cooperative close

Both nodes agree on the final balance and sign one new **closing transaction** that pays each side directly. There are no time locks, the fee is small and agreed, and both get their money as soon as it confirms. This is how a channel *should* end.

<Exercise id="ch09-coop-close" />

## Force close

If the other side is offline, unresponsive or misbehaving, a node can <Term id="force-close" />: it publishes its latest <Term id="commitment-transaction" /> on its own. Then:

- the other side is paid immediately,
- the publisher's own output waits behind its time lock (`blocks_til_maturity`), and
- any HTLCs still in flight are settled on-chain, each with its own deadline.

<Exercise id="ch09-force-close" />

Dina's funds now show as `limbo_balance` with a count of blocks to go. On regtest you can mine them away; on mainnet that wait could be a day or more.
