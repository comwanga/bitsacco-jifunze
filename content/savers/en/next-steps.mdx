You now know what money does, what sats are, who holds the keys, how Lightning payments work, how to spot a scam, and how a chama can save together. That is more than most people know about Bitcoin.

## Read the source

The Bitcoin <Term id="whitepaper" /> is only nine pages long, and it is available in Kiswahili. You won't understand every line, and that's fine. Read the introduction and the conclusion first.

For depth, **Kujifunza Bitcoin** is the Kiswahili edition of *Mastering Bitcoin*. It is a long, technical book; dip into the chapters on keys, wallets and transactions when you want to go further. Both are in the [library](/en/library/).

When a word puzzles you, check the [glossary](/en/glossary/).

## Teach someone

The best way to remember something is to explain it. Pick one idea, such as the difference between an address and a seed phrase, and teach it to a family member or at your next chama meeting. If they can explain it back to you, you both learned it.

## Keep going

- **Curious about how it works inside?** The [builders path](/en/builders/) starts from the basics of a developer's lab.
- **Saving with others?** Explore Bitsacco's savings tools for individuals and chamas at bitsacco.com.
- **Want to help?** Jifunze is <Term id="open-source" />. Translations, corrections and new lessons arrive as pull requests on GitHub, and every contribution is reviewed.
