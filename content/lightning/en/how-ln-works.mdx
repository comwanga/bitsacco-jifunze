Every <Term id="channel" /> lives through three moves.

1. **Open.** One side sends bitcoin to a 2-of-2 <Term id="multisig" /> output that needs both signatures to spend. This *funding transaction* is an ordinary Bitcoin transaction.
2. **Update.** For each payment, both sides sign a new <Term id="commitment-transaction" /> that splits the funds differently. Nobody broadcasts it: it just sits ready, and the old one is revoked.
3. **Close.** When they are done, the latest split goes on-chain, either together (a cooperative close) or by one side alone.

Only moves 1 and 3 touch the blockchain. Everything in between is just messages.
