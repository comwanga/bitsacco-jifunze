## Discussion-based learning

The required open-ended activities draw pedagogical inspiration from [Chaincode Labs' Bitcoin Protocol Development seminar](https://chaincode.gitbook.io/seminars/bitcoin-protocol-development), including its topic readings, design-trade-off discussions and learner-generated questions. Jifunze's questions and additional explanations are newly authored for its audiences, not reproductions or translations of Chaincode's question bank. Links do not imply endorsement or permission to relicense third-party material. The [curriculum guide](docs/content-guide.md) maps the technical topics and research sources.

## Mastering the Lightning Network

The 17 lessons in `content/lightning/` are adapted, simplified and translated into Kiswahili from [*Mastering the Lightning Network*](https://github.com/lnbook/lnbook) by Andreas M. Antonopoulos, Olaoluwa Osuntokun and René Pickhardt, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each lesson names the book and its chapter in `source` and resources. The lab's four-node topology (Alice, Bob, Chan and Dina) follows the book's examples.

## Lab software

The lab in `lab/` runs regtest node images from [Polar](https://github.com/jamaljsr/polar). Polar's image definitions are MIT-licensed; bundled Bitcoin Core, LND, Core Lightning and Eclair software retains its respective upstream license. The bridge, CLI wrappers, helpers and checks are Jifunze code (MIT).

Jifunze publishes a PDF generated from the translation's open-source text, with an in-app reader and download; the [provenance record](docs/deployment/kujifunza-bitcoin-generated.md) and [source manifest](public/books/kujifunza-bitcoin-sw-3rd-edition.source.json) identify it. ## Mastering Bitcoin — generated English PDF

[*Mastering Bitcoin: Programming the Open Blockchain*, third edition](https://github.com/bitcoinbook/bitcoinbook), by Andreas M. Antonopoulos and David A. Harding, is locally hosted as a PDF generated from official open-source revision `275c4eb8eab8800c6adc39f8def8e8f8fa356a57`, under CC BY-SA 4.0. It is not the publisher's official PDF. Typesetting, open fonts, linked index, bookmarks and provenance notices were added; book content was not rewritten.

The [provenance and reproduction record](docs/deployment/mastering-bitcoin-generated.md) documents the later license grant, asset credits and completeness checks. The [source manifest](public/books/mastering-bitcoin-en-3rd-edition.source.json) records the exact artifact checksum. Separate MIT whitepaper, CC0 errata, CC BY 4.0 Bitcoin Design Guide images, [font](scripts/books/font-notices.txt) and [MathJax](scripts/books/mathjax-LICENSE.txt) notices are retained in the PDF.
