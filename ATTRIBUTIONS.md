All lessons in `content/` are original Bitsacco Jifunze lessons, released under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Any lesson adapted from another work names its source and licence in its `source` front matter and is listed here.

## Mastering the Lightning Network

The 17 lessons in `content/lightning/` are adapted, simplified and translated into Kiswahili from [*Mastering the Lightning Network*](https://github.com/lnbook/lnbook) by Andreas M. Antonopoulos, Olaoluwa Osuntokun and René Pickhardt, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each lesson names the book and its chapter in `source` and resources. The lab's four-node topology (Alice, Bob, Chan and Dina) follows the book's examples.

## Lab software

The lab in `lab/` runs [Polar](https://github.com/jamaljsr/polar)'s regtest node images (MIT) for Bitcoin Core, LND, Core Lightning and Eclair. The bridge, CLI wrappers, helpers and checks are Jifunze code (MIT).

## Learning-flow inspiration

The capstone and community-review flow at the end of the builders path was inspired by [TheBuidl](https://www.thebuidl.xyz/) cohort programmes (learn, capstone project, mentorship). No TheBuidl material is copied.
