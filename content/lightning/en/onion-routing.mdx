When Alice pays Dina through Bob and Chan, Bob needs to know where to send the payment next, but not that it's going to Dina. <Term id="onion-routing" /> gives each <Term id="node" /> exactly what it needs and nothing more.

## Layers, like an onion

Alice knows the whole route. She builds a message in layers, innermost first:

1. The **Dina** layer: "you are the final hop; the amount is 21,000."
2. Wrapped in the **Chan** layer: "forward 21,000 to Dina."
3. Wrapped in the **Bob** layer: "forward 21,001 to Chan."

Each layer is encrypted with a key only that hop can work out. Bob peels his layer, reads "forward to Chan", and passes on the rest, which to him is random-looking bytes. Chan peels the next one. Only Dina's layer says "it's for you".

## What real onions add (Sphinx)

- **Fixed size.** Every onion is 1,366 bytes, padded with filler, so a hop can't count the remaining hops.
- **Per-hop shared secrets.** Alice uses a fresh ephemeral key per payment; each hop derives its secret with ECDH, and the key is blinded at every step so hops can't link the onion they received with the one they send on.
- **HMACs.** Each layer carries an integrity tag. A tampered onion is rejected, and the error travels back encrypted, layer by layer, to Alice.
